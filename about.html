ABOUT

Ethan Pervere is a Connecticut-based scenic and sound designer, draftsperson, and AV technician working across theatre and live events.
Their scenic design for Carrie: The Musical received Excellence in Scenic Design at the 2024 Kennedy Center American College Theater Festival Region 1 and was selected to present at the national festival in Washington, D.C. Recent work includes serving as the Sound Supervisor for Deceived and The Understudy at the Dorset Theatre Festival, Audio/Visual Apprentice at The Glimmerglass Festival in 2024, overhire staff for Hartford Stage, TheaterWorks Hartford, and a stagehand for IATSE Local 84.
Ethan holds a B.A. in New Media Studies (Performance Media) and a B.S. in Computer Science from Eastern Connecticut State University. Their work explores storytelling, sound environments, and emerging technology to create cohesive live experiences.
