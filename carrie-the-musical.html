Design Concept

The scenic design encompasses Carrie's life and the world she creates for herself as she struggles against her peers and her emerging abilities. Using familiar school objects, the performers transform the stage into several locations, ranging from a small-town high school to Carrie's home. From the moment the audience enters the theater, the scenic painting, towering staircase, and carefully selected props immerse them in Carrie's world and foreshadow the events to come.
